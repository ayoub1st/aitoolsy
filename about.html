About Us

Why AI Toolsy exists, and what we're actually trying to build.

We're a small group of young people who kept noticing the same problem from completely different directions: everyone around us was searching for the same kind of thing, an AI, a tool, an application, that could actually help with something specific, and almost nobody was finding a straightforward way to compare their options.

What we found instead was noise. Plenty of tool-makers were happy to promote their own product and call it the best on the market, whether or not that was true. Others leaned into exaggeration or outright misleading claims simply to pull in views and downloads. Caught in the middle was the person just trying to figure out which AI tool actually did what they needed, at a price they could live with.

So we tried something different

Instead of building one more tool, or one more app promoting itself, we built a place that promotes nothing but the truth about what's already out there. The idea was simple: gather everything, every category, every use case, every kind of AI tool we could verify, into one platform, in one place, free to use, and free to come back to whenever you need it. No subscription, no paywall, no catch.

We also didn't want this to be a one-way street. If you think something's missing, wrong, or outdated, you can talk to us directly, through the Support section of this site, and ask us to add it, fix it, or reconsider it. We take that seriously and read everything with an open mind. This directory gets better because people tell us when it's wrong, not because we assume we already got it right.

Why "AI Toolsy"

We wanted a name that felt approachable rather than corporate, something that sounds like a companion for navigating this space, not another intimidating tech brand. "AI" speaks for itself; "Toolsy" was our way of keeping the whole thing feel personal and a little informal, the way we'd actually talk about it with a friend who asked, "is there an AI for that?" There usually is, and now there's one place to find it.

Where we're headed

This is very much a living project, not a finished product. We're actively working on translating more of the site into the languages already available in the menu, expanding categories as new kinds of AI tools emerge, and, as we mention in our FAQ, eventually bringing a proper mobile app into the mix. Every piece of feedback we get shapes what we prioritize next.

If that sounds like something you'd want to be part of shaping, we'd genuinely like to hear from you. Thanks for spending time here.
